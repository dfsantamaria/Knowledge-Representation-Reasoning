Competency Questions

The CQs (Competency Questions) reveal that the model still contains some ambiguity. In particular, the association between Film, Employee, and Role is ambiguous, even though the GPs are successfully satisfied.

More specifically, the current model does not allow us to identify which role an employee performs in each individual film.

This highlights a limitation in the current conceptualisation: although the model may satisfy the GPs, it does not fully capture the semantics required to answer all the CQs correctly.

Therefore, a refactoring is needed to eliminate the ambiguity. The proposed solution is presented in V2.


-Functional CQs:
--fCQ1: Given a film, the list of people who worked on it and their respective roles (e.g., actor, director).
---GP: Film->Commitment->Person; Commitment → Role → (Actor, Director)
--fCQ2: Given an actor, the list of films they have participated in
---GP: Commitment -> Role -> Actor; Commitment → Film
--fCQ3: Given a director, the list of films they have directed.
---GP: Commitment -> Role -> Director; Commitment → Film
--fCQ4: Given a company, the list of employees and the roles they performed.
---GP: Company → Commitment -> Film; Commitment → Person; Commitment → Role


-Supplementary CQs:
--sCQ2: Given a genre, the list of people who worked on it and their respective roles (e.g., actor, director).
---GP: Genre->Film -> Commitment->Person; Commitment → Role → (Actor, Director) 


-Quality CQs:
--qCQ1: Chech whether a film is a remake.
---GP: EMPTY
--qCQ2: Find the employee that worked both as director and actor in the same film.
---GP: Commitment -> Role-> Actor; Commitment-> Role-> Director; Commitment -> Film
--qCQ3: Which is the most frequent keyword.
---GP: MAX(COUNT-DISTINCT(KEYWORD -> FILM))

-Coverage CQs:
--gCQ1: Check whether a person worked for a given a company.
---GP: Person->Commitment->Film; Commitment->Company
--gCQ2: Find the maximum cost for a film given a company
---GP: MAX(Commitment->Film->Cost; Commitment->Company)

-Complementary CQs:
--cCQ1: Given an employee, the list of films they have worked on and the roles they performed in each film.
--GP: Person → Commitment → Film; Commitment → Role
