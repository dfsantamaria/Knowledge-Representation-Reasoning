-Goal: representing the information related to films and employees.
-Purposes: 
---Production Department	manages film projects, production schedules and assigned employees.
---Human Resources (HR)	manages employee details, contracts, roles and salaries.
---Casting Department	searches for actors and assigns them to film roles.
---Direction Department	identifies directors and tracks their film projects.
---Accounting Department manages employee salaries, film budgets and production costs.
---Marketing Department	retrieves film titles, genres, release dates and directors for promotion.
---Distribution Department	manages film releases, distribution information and availability.


User Story
A company recruits personnel for a film production

Each film is characterized by the following information:
- $GENRE: one or more film genres to which the film belongs;
- $TITLE: the film's title;
- $COST: the total production cost;
- $RUNTIME: the film's duration, expressed in minutes;
- $YEAR: the year of production;
- $LANG: the film's original language;
- $KEYWORD: a set of keywords, consisting of nouns, used to facilitate the search and identification of the film in databases.

The company $COMPANY commits ($COMMITMENT) several professional figures $EMPLOYEE for a $FILM. These figures correspond to individuals $PERSON who perform specific roles $ROLE within the $COMMITMENT.

The roles involved include:
- $DIRECTOR: the person responsible for directing the film and coordinating the filming process;
- $ACTOR: the person who portrays one or more characters $CHARACTER in the film.


Open Questions:
-> What is the relationship between $GENRE and $KEYWORD?
-> Are there shared vocabularies for well-known nouns?
