# Knowledge Representation and Reasoning

**Department of Mathematics and Computer Science**  
**University of Catania**  
Master's Degree in Computer Science (LM-18)

## Scope

This repository contains teaching and learning materials for the course **Knowledge Representation and Reasoning**, offered by the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at the University of Catania.

The repository may include lecture notes, slides, exercises, examples, and other educational resources related to the course.
